CEF Pakistan Portal V24

This version corrects the reporting and download requirements.

1. CEF PROMOTION contains only:
   - CEF promotional meetings
   - Personal visits to promote CEF
   - CEF conferences

   Its report form asks for:
   - People Reached
   - People Now Involved in CEF Ministry
   It does NOT ask for children reached/accepted.

2. PRAYER MEETINGS is a separate ministry section and does not use children metrics.
   It records people reached and people now involved in CEF ministry.

3. TEACHING MINISTRY remains teacher-focused:
   - Total People/Teachers Trained
   - How many are now involved in CEF ministry
   - No children-reached fields.

4. Staff downloads:
   - Monthly, quarterly, annual and complete Excel
   - Monthly, quarterly, annual and complete A4 PDF
   - Excel contains CEF logo and staff information
   - PDF contains CEF logo and staff information

5. ND downloads:
   - Ministry directory Excel/PDF with staff information
   - National ministry report Excel/PDF with staff information attached to each ministry record
   - A4 PDF format

6. Login page is preserved.

Setup:
- Run V24_SETUP.sql once in Supabase SQL Editor.
- Then use the portal files in this ZIP.
- Do not delete existing staff or ND accounts.
