CEF Pakistan Portal - Ministry & Budget Overview Addition

This package is based on the uploaded V37 baseline. Existing features and files were preserved.
Only the following ND feature was added:
- Separate ND sidebar: Ministry Overview
- Ministry totals for Prayer Meetings, Teaching Ministry and CEF Promotion
- Region, city/district, individual staff and date-range filters
- Ministry Overview PDF and Excel downloads with CEF branding and ND signature
- Separate ND sidebar: Budget Overview
- Total requested and approved budget
- Region, city/district, individual staff and date-range filters
- Budget Overview PDF and Excel downloads with CEF branding and ND signature

No new SQL migration is required because the overview reads existing ministry_reports,
staff_registration_requests and staff_budget_applications data.
